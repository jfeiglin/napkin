## S5-E: the engine

You own what S4b-E owned. Run declared solvers on the solve library:
- Lower a solver's sections to `napkin/engine/solve/` problems: unknowns (flows, projected values
  of members, other values), affine rows, ranked objectives, claims and targets; one `Solver` per
  run (sync by keys, so members that join and leave add and delete columns and rows).
- At a moment: once the round's writes, starts and flows that join or leave are registered, each
  affected solver is solved once, in dependency order, and its results are published together;
  then conditions are read (spec, "Rounds"; the example `two_writes_one_solve`). A read of a result
  before it settles is an error that names the result. An infeasible or failed solve stops the run
  before a dependent trigger or report sees a partial result, naming the step (`SolveFailure`).
- Between moments: a solver whose inputs do not move holds its answer; one whose inputs move is
  solved at trial states inside a small component, and refused by name at scale (D3 asks that a
  large valid model run or stop with an explicit failure, never freeze a moving rate: say which
  limit you chose and why).
- Local sharing at endpoints outside a solver stays as it is; a declared solver owns its claims.

Tests: each of the 17 examples against its values and the oracle (add them to `SUBSET` of
`tests/engine/test_programs.py`); both halves of "once in a round"; a stale read; an infeasible
solve; members that join and leave a solve; a generator of small random programs with a solver
compared with the oracle (a hundred cases in a normal run, more under `deep`).

Carried over from S4, still refused "(S4)" or "(S5)" (`b1/s4a-engine/STATE.md`,
`b1/s4b-engine/STATE.md`): in a stage, the assignment of a formula or a collection; keyed reads of
moving values and keys; a setting that reads an actual rate or a served fraction of its own set, or
of a set whose rates move between moments (decide from the spec whether such a model runs or needs a
declared solver, and refuse it by name if it does).

Last, if usage allows: the dormant engine files of earlier slices (`test_runtime.py` and
