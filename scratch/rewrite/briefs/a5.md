# Brief A5: catalogue fit

Folder: `scratch/rewrite/a5/`. Read `briefs/COMMON.md` first.

## Question

Does the new language cover the real models? How much of their migration is mechanical? What does
a check of a migrated model have to compare?

## Read

- `scratch/rewrite/spec.md`, all of it. For this brief: "How templates compose into a run", "A run
  produces one trajectory", "Model text, status cases and evidence", "Evidence at the point of
  use", "Actor decisions and observations", "Numerical scale hints", "Assertions and their timing",
  "Reference behavior in the current compiler", "Expressions and instantaneous transfers", "Shared
  resources and competing invocations", "Open design questions".
- At the base commit: `docs/language.md` in full (the present language). The models:
  `examples/piano_tuners/model.scn`, `examples/reservoir/model.scn`,
  `scenarios/water_controls_wipe/water.scn` with `model.scn` and its `variants/`,
  `scenarios/heat_wave_blackout/model.scn`, `scenarios/iv_fluid_shortage/model.scn`,
  `scenarios/cyber_learning_loop/model.scn`.
- The old compiler, with `PYTHONPATH=/home/user/napkin-base`: `napkin.model.language` (the parser
  gives a `nodes.File`), and `napkin.model.model.build_model(path)` (the compiled model, with its
  structures and what it proved, such as the cumulative outputs).

## Tasks

1. **Census, with the old parser and never with a text pattern.** For each `.scn` file of
   `examples/` and `scenarios/`, count each kind of construct: declarations by kind; flows (plain,
   with legs, with `while`, in a `share`); events by trigger kind, with windows, with `while` on
   the header, with outcomes and chances, with choices; `status` lines; `assert`, with and without
   `within`; outputs with their settings; options; runs, with and without `versus`; `use`,
   `variant`, `change`; records, claims and notes; `sees`; actors; tables; and each function that
   an expression calls (`since`, `count`, `elapsed`, `delayed`, `each`, `distinct`, a `sum` over a
   list, `min` or `max` with an extra argument). Write `census.md` and `census.json`.
2. **Translations by hand**, in `translated/`: both `examples/` models in full;
   `water_controls_wipe/water.scn` in full with one variant (its shares, its flows with legs, its
   crew pools and its stages are the main risk); the options and the runs of `heat_wave_blackout`,
   with one actor that has a choice, and its status cases; one group of runs with `versus` from
   `cyber_learning_loop`. Where the spec has no spelling for a construct, propose one in the style
   of the spec, mark the line `# PROPOSED`, and list it.
3. **A migration table**, `migration-table.md`: each old construct of the census, and its new
   form, or the decision that it needs, or "no form in the first version".
4. **`translate.py`**: with the old parser, translate the lines that are mechanical: values with
   their units, ranges, records and texts; inputs; records; notes; tables. Mark the rest
   `# TODO(hand)`. Print, for each file, the share of the lines that it translated.
5. **`check.py OLD NEW`**: compare a translation with its old file. It must check meaning, and not
   only words:
   - each number with its unit, and each text, of the old file is in the new one;
   - each name keeps its kind: a store, an accumulated tally (a stock that flows only fill), a
     rate, a formula, an input, a state, an output;
   - it lists each output that the old compiler proved cumulative. The new compiler must later
     prove that each of them never falls.

   No new compiler exists yet, so read the new file with a simple reader of your own. Run the check
   on your own translations. One real case that it must catch: in the water model the unserved rate
   (Mgal/h) falls to zero when service returns, and the unserved volume (Mgal) accumulates that
   rate and keeps the loss (`water.scn:264`, `:374`, `:701`). A translation that turns that volume
   into a formula of the present shortfall must fail the check.
6. **Where a comparison lives now.** The spec removes `versus`: one run gives one trajectory, and a
   comparison is made outside. See how `tools/publish` and the briefs use `versus`, the named runs
   and the files `expected-orders.json` and `brief.toml` today. Propose where the named runs and
   the comparisons live in the new language and package. Give two alternatives and a
   recommendation.

## Rulings that you owe

A spelling for each thing that the spec gives none: the scenario line (title, horizon, scope,
library, timescales); options and named runs; a comparison; `use`, `variant` and `change`, with
their precedence and their conflicts; records and claims; notes; `sees`; the chance producer and
the choice producer of a `switch`; the report selection and a default `report:` block; scale hints
(`size`, `resolution`). The fate of each old form that has no new one. Evidence for a value that an
option or a `change` gives.

## Exit

`census.md`, `census.json`, `translated/`, `migration-table.md`, `translate.py`, `check.py`,
`FINDINGS.md`. Each old form has a new form or a ruling.
