`two_writes_one_solve`) is refused by name with "(S5)".

**Part one (S4a): occurrences and time.**
- Events; `.start(...)` from the setup and from a stage, its arguments captured left to right when
  the statement runs and checked against the parameters (type, unit, entry bounds); a scalar
  parameter is a copy and an entity parameter a reference (D13); a start on each member of a
  collection (spec, "Calling an event on a collection").
- Locals, and an occurrence's own stores and flows, initialized in written order (D14).
- `after D:`, `after D when C:` (C read once when the stage comes due), the wait `after D`, the gate
  `until C`; `if`/`else`; `switch` on a computed enum value, with case guards and `else`.
- `=`, `+=` and `-=` on any writable field: of `this`, through a link or an entity parameter, of a
  live occurrence. A finished occurrence is frozen, and a write to it is an error; `readonly`
  refuses direct assignment. `move` (spec, "Expressions and instantaneous transfers").
- The moment: stages due together commit one after another in the established order (D5), then
  flows join and leave, local sharing settles, and conditions are read; another round follows at
  the same time while work is due.
- Triggers: one per receiver and run; each fires when its condition first becomes true (a discrete
  condition at committed changes; a continuous one at its first contact with an inclusive boundary,
  also between moments, worked out again whenever what it reads changes, a flow that joins or
  leaves included); `reset when`, `reset after`, captures. A strict continuous threshold, or a
  first contact that the engine cannot certify, is a diagnostic, never polling.
- Occurrences: `.occurrences` with `started`, `done`, `active` and a declared `status`; `.at(t)` (the
  newest start; of two at one time, the one created later); `count`, `any`; `since(e)`. A finished
  occurrence is a stable record (D7); its flows leave their endpoints' collections together, read
  0 and keep their last request and capacity.
- Retirement (an occurrence's stores are empty at normal completion, or the run fails and names the
  stock, the amount, the unit, the occurrence and the time); the horizon as an inclusive cutoff;
  the limits of D6 and their diagnostics.

**Part two (S4b): chance, choices and checks.**
- `.draw()` in a stage or a local, keyed by its site and by the member or occurrence that executes
  it; `sampled` and `median`; a `switch` on `categorical(...).draw()` with case guards (spec,
  "Choosing actions inside a stage"); distribution-valued inputs and their overrides (D2).
- Actors: `choose(d)`, the controllers and picks of the configuration, `sees`, and the pause of one
  atomic stage: a prompt never makes a second draw, child or transfer.
- `assert` over state (spec, "Assertions and their timing"); `elapsed` and `delayed`.
