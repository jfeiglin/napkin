# Taking over from a stopped agent

You continue the work of an agent that was stopped. The account moved its agents to another model
to save usage. The work of the earlier agent is on disk; its working memory is not. Do this:

1. Read `briefs/COMMON.md`, then your own brief, in full.
2. Read `STATE.md` in your folder. The earlier agent wrote it for you: what is done and in which
   file, each decision and why, what it was in the middle of, the steps that remain, and what it
   learned that is in no other file.
3. Look at the files of the folder as far as you need them. Run an existing script before you
   change it, so that you know what works.
4. Read the parts of `scratch/rewrite/spec.md` that your remaining steps need. Do not write a
   ruling without the text of the spec that it rests on.
5. Go on from the steps that remain. Do not do finished work again. If you find finished work
   wrong, mend it and say so in `FINDINGS.md`.
6. Usage is short. Read what you need. Do not make again a long measurement that `STATE.md` already
   records, unless the exit of your brief needs it. Write what the brief asks, and no more.
7. Keep `STATE.md` up to date at each milestone: it is one small edit.

The exit criterion, the form of `FINDINGS.md` and the last message are those of your brief and of
`COMMON.md`.
