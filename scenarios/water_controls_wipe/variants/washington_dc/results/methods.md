# water_controls_wipe:washington_dc methods: Water systems' controls wiped through a shared remote-support platform: Washington, DC

An AI agent wipes the controls of the water systems of Washington, DC at once, through the remote-support platform of the OT integrator that they share: the Washington Aqueduct, DC Water, Arlington, Fairfax Water and WSSC Water.

This page describes the model of this scenario and its pathways.
Model version: `f1661311aa7e3897dc37b2d620f16bd8492712281aeb6572fc634be460755c17`. Engine version: `0.0.1`.

## The attack and who it affects

AI operation wipes the controls of the water systems of a place through their integrator's platform.

The calculation starts when the AI operation chosen for this scenario takes over the remote-support platform of an OT integrator and wipes the controls of the water systems of the place behind it. The shared platform, the systems it reaches, the depth of the wipe and the hidden credential are inputs that the scenario chooses and states. Published incidents put internet-exposed water-sector controllers out of action, intruders kept valid accounts on control networks, and a 2026 wave of incidents reached several systems in one state without taking their water out of service; none of them wiped the controls of many utilities through one shared platform. That reach is what this scenario assumes.

The people the systems of the place serve.

The first AI cyber action starts the incident and the clocks of the response.

An AI agent for a hostile group. It aims to leave as many people as possible without water for as long as it can. It holds the remote-support platform of an OT integrator and, through it, the controllers of the water systems of a place. Its first move wipes the controls of every system it reaches at once. With a hidden credential that the first cleanup misses, it watches the crews restore the controls and wipes them again within minutes of each restoration, until responders remove the credential.

Each pathway combines one choice of Response with one option. The model counts people,
materials, services and money in the units of the scenario. When a quantity moves, what
leaves one place arrives at the next. Shared resources go to their users by priority or in
fixed shares, as the scenario sets.

The scenario holds these conditions true. Where one does not hold, the results do not apply.

## Limits of the results

The reviewers of this scenario state these limits.

- Customers get their normal use while usable stored water remains, and only what is produced or received once it is gone. Real pressure falls before storage empties and differs between zones, which hydraulic models represent with pressure-dependent demand. The reduced form here needs local calibration before any utility reads its own service from it.
- The hours of controls, field, replacement and clearance work for each system, the crews' capacity, the stage times of mutual aid and the share of production that manual operation keeps are choices for this scenario, by the kind of system. Incident records give elapsed times, not hours of qualified work, and no national record gives the qualified crews available at once.
- Each place is one where a part of the scenario's evidence comes from, with the systems that the evidence names and the systems that sell water to them. The places are not a sample of the country, a result for one place says nothing about another, and the systems of a place need not share an integrator in fact.
- Each system's plants, storage, use and sales come from its own reports, which differ in year, in what they cover and in how they count. The research note of each place says which figure each value takes and why, and which figures could not be read in full.
- The share of activity that businesses keep and the length of their recovery come from a Swedish survey of stated impacts of hypothetical outages, applied to US areas. The value added of the area that a system serves is a share of its counties' value added by people served, and its dependence on public water is a choice. Effects on other areas and the cost of the response are not counted, and a regional study's monthly loss estimate is a benchmark, not a validation.
- Deaths and serious casualties are not estimated and are not zero. The model reports the hospitals' facility-hours short of water. The degradation of services reported within hours of a water loss comes from voluntary facility assessments and is not a mortality relationship.
- Which system supplies a hospital comes from the EPA service areas, which are modelled for many systems and can be wrong at their edges. Every hospital has the same reserve and critical need. A hospital with its own well or a second supply keeps more water than the model gives it.
- The 2026 incidents in Minnesota and Georgia, the 2024 incident at a national water company and the population of the city whose incident the article cites motivate the study; they do not set its severity. A multi-system episode does not identify a national share losing water, an advisory is not proof of contamination, a billing outage is not a loss of service, and a utility serves residents, not the people its web page counts.
- The Aqueduct's 49 Mgal sit in three reservoirs in DC Water's zones, but the model keeps them with the Aqueduct for all three buyers. So Arlington and Fairfax Water can keep water longer here than in fact, and DC Water can lose it sooner.
- The peak records of WSSC Water (1999) and Fairfax Water (2010) are old, and WSSC Water's recent highest days are lower: 222.2 Mgal in 2020. So the stored water of these systems can run out sooner here than in fact.

## How the harm is calculated and checked

[The research record](../evidence/research.md) names the patient group, the source populations, the
outcome and its timing, the source values applied to this scenario, the economic accounts
and the local measurements that are still necessary.
Water is counted once as stored water or room for it, and as delivered, lost, sent on or short; work is counted once as still to do or done; economic loss adds the value added lost in the areas that the systems serve less what is made up, in US dollars at 2025 prices. Deaths and serious casualties are not estimated and are not zero.
Each complete report keeps its inputs, its values at each reporting time and the versions used.

## Independent checks and how to repeat the results

Independent checks in the [test suite](../../../../../tests) calculate the mathematics again, check that
no quantity is lost or created, and test the schedules and the cases that must fail.
[Evidence checks](../../../../docs/evidence/README.md) show how each source was found, read and matched to the text
that it supports. [Review records](../../../../docs/evidence/reviews/README.md) list the scientific and model reviews
and what each one decided.

The Radau solver uses the time step that the scenario sets. Parts of the model that
are proved to be linear use exact solutions. Reports keep the full calculated precision; the
pages round the values for display.

Run `python -B -m tools.check` to check the software. Run
`napkin run scenarios/water_controls_wipe --variant washington_dc` to run the scenario again; `--set NAME=VALUE` and
`--edits FILE` change its inputs.
