# cyber_learning_loop methods: A country's cyber attacks and the learning loop

A country splits its scarce cyber operators between surveillance and attacks, and its researchers and analysts serve both. Surveillance pays each day that it runs, but an attack pays only when the country uses it, and the use burns its flaws and tools. Attacks teach the country only when it makes many of them, and the lessons fade. The model follows the split, the flaws, the lessons and the results for 25 years while AI makes attacks cheaper. It shows when military plans start to count on cyber attacks.

This page describes the model of this scenario and its pathways.
Model version: `ffe42fe4a0b819ef40eb012ffc73d0979d7e658c44145a452ca29bfcf3f3fd2e`. Engine version: `0.0.1`.

## The attack and who it affects

Attacks of the country's cyber force.

The model starts in 2026 with today's split of the experts. Today's split is a rest point of the funding rule, so without AI the force stays where it starts. The numbers of today come from the United States, the one country with a public split of money and people.

The outcome definitions name the people or services that the scenario counts.

The AI operation starts with its first events. Whether each attack action happens or is blocked decides what follows.

The country's cyber force when it makes attacks: its experts, its access and its tools. An AI agent does more of the work as AI improves.

Each pathway combines one choice of Response with one option. The model counts people,
materials, services and money in the units of the scenario. When a quantity moves, what
leaves one place arrives at the next. Shared resources go to their users by priority or in
fixed shares, as the scenario sets.

## Limits of the results

The reviewers of this scenario state these limits.

- The model follows one country. Other countries' attacks, and a race between countries, change the attention of defenders and the value of attacks in ways that the model does not follow.
- Value is in units of one expert-year of surveillance in 2026. The model compares the two missions; it gives no price of either.
- Most values are assumptions of this scenario, with ranges that a reader can explore. A range is not a measured interval.

## Independent checks and how to repeat the results

Independent checks in the [test suite](../../../tests) calculate the mathematics again, check that
no quantity is lost or created, and test the schedules and the cases that must fail.
[Evidence checks](../../docs/evidence/README.md) show how each source was found, read and matched to the text
that it supports. [Review records](../../docs/evidence/reviews/README.md) list the scientific and model reviews
and what each one decided.

The Radau solver uses the time step that the scenario sets. Parts of the model that
are proved to be linear use exact solutions. Reports keep the full calculated precision; the
pages round the values for display.

Run `python -B -m tools.check` to check the software. Run
`napkin run scenarios/cyber_learning_loop` to run the scenario again; `--set NAME=VALUE` and
`--edits FILE` change its inputs.
